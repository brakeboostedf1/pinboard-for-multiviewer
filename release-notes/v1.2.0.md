First public release.

- Up to 4 boards of MultiViewer players in one window.
- Change board with the edge arrows, the title bar dots or the keyboard.
- Resize the window: players scale with it and stay inside.
- F11: full screen, with board dots at the bottom.
- Board names, slide speed and shortcuts in Settings.
- Player menu: find a lost or minimized player and bring it back.
- When you close Pinboard: keep the players open, or close them too.
- Video players show black in screen captures.
