First public release.

- Up to 4 boards of MultiViewer players and timing windows in one window. One board shows at a time.
- Change board with the edge arrows, the board dots at the top of the screen, or the keyboard.
- Players stay where your MultiViewer setup puts them. Save a setup in MultiViewer: it opens with each player on its board.
- The board dots and name fade out when a player is under them. Touch the top edge of the screen to show them again.
- The board fills the screen, with the taskbar visible. F11: the board also covers the taskbar.
- Player menu: find a lost or minimized player and bring it back.
- Board names, slide speed and shortcuts in Settings.
- When you close Pinboard: keep the players open, or close them too.
- Video players show black in screen captures. Timing windows stay visible.
